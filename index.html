Origins & evolution

Several investigations.
One business case.

A business description can raise several different questions. Who supplies the business? What happens if a critical supplier fails? Which competitors matter? What regulatory activity needs investigation? Each question needs its own research, while the final assessment needs to account for how those answers depend on each other.

At YHack 2026, our team built TRIAGE to coordinate due diligence, stress testing, competitive intelligence and compliance analysis. The prototype gave those functions separate workflows and brought them together in a synthesized report. That division of work remains the foundation of TRIAGE+.

What needed to change

The prototype could produce specialist reports, but its JSON contracts and citation formats varied between agents. Model interpretations could acquire confidence as they passed between stages. Reports primarily lived in browser state, making long investigations difficult to inspect after a failure.

TRIAGE+ adds a common finding contract around the existing specialists. Each finding carries an identity, claim class, evidence references and uncertainty. Reconciliation applies deterministic rules before a finding can affect the report. Stored snapshots and job checkpoints make the investigation inspectable beyond the current browser session.

The workstation

A case file you can inspect.

The interface follows a laboratory workstation: a persistent case header, compact metadata, ruled registers and status readouts. IBM Plex Sans carries the application text; monospace is reserved for identifiers and measurements. Light mode is the default. Color distinguishes actions, evidence classes and execution states.

The original interface centered on submitting a description. TRIAGE+ gives the resulting case a stable place to work: specialist results, source records, a reconciled report and an archive share the same navigation.

Execution graph

Specialists inside a controlled workflow.

FastAPI schedules explicit dependencies. Due diligence establishes the initial context; stress testing, competitive intelligence and compliance then run as parallel branches. These are programmed handoffs. The model does not choose an arbitrary execution plan.

Shared execution services

Lava supplies OpenAI-compatible inference; Tavily supplies retrieval. Central clients bound retries, reject malformed responses and record attempts against the investigation budget.

Structured results & persistence

Each specialist is wrapped in a validated finding contract. SQLite stores report snapshots, job checkpoints and usage records; the workstation reads those persisted results.

The final report first gathers and revalidates specialist findings. A model may propose cross-domain connections, which face further validation; executive prioritization selects existing finding IDs. Deterministic scoring uses structured severity, likelihood and evidence confidence. Those inputs remain estimates, so a transparent formula does not make the score a calibrated probability.

Evidence integrity

What does this statement actually establish?

A citation can be real while the attached claim is unsupported. The October hardening pass tightened the observational gate: the whole claim statement must be reproduced from a valid retrieved quotation. A shortened sentence that drops a condition or negation cannot pass on model confidence alone.

Claim class is separate from evidence availability. A search failure, an empty search result and insufficient support carry different states. The report can therefore retain a severe simulated risk without presenting it as a documented incident.

Provenance has to survive the handoff

Source records carry IDs, URLs, excerpts and fingerprints. Shared hosts, upstream references and bounded near-duplicate matching help group sources that should not count as independent. The current matcher compares five-word shingles in the first 500 words; mirrored excerpts with changed headers can still be grouped. Heavily paraphrased syndication and common ownership remain difficult.

Known dependencies constrain downstream claim type and confidence. Contradiction checks normalize a bounded vocabulary, common exact quantities and units, and year/FY notation. Conflicted findings are excluded from scoring; related actions become investigations. Currency conversion, ranges and arbitrary semantic paraphrases fall outside those comparisons.

Legal context stays explicit

Findings can record relevant activity, triggering conditions, applicability source references and effective dates. Missing context keeps applicability potential or unknown. Legal dates require authoritative quoted support and a valid date. These gates do not establish an authoritative legal conclusion.

The deliberate trade-off

Exact textual reproduction is inspectable, but conservative. Useful paraphrases may be downgraded. Incorrect sources, mistaken entity attribution and context outside an excerpt can still pass a quotation check. Semantic entailment and source truth remain unresolved evaluation problems.

Execution & failure

A long investigation needs a record of what happened.

An investigation may finish due diligence and market mapping before a later provider call fails. Checkpoints preserve completed stages, while parallel branches isolate failures. A failed mapping stage cannot start deeper research against provisional candidate names.

Automatic starts accept an idempotency key. A repeated matching request returns the stored job; different input with the same key is rejected. Canceled and interrupted keys also return their existing records, avoiding an accidental new billable investigation.

Cancellation is cooperative. New provider requests stop; an accepted remote call may finish and incur cost. Admission remains held while work drains. On restart, unfinished jobs become INTERRUPTED; history restores the saved results without silently replaying execution.

Configurable local defaults. Parallel specialist threads share an investigation budget. Separate guided API requests are separate operations.

Token reservations use input byte length plus the requested completion allowance; returned provider usage replaces the estimate when available. Unknown usage retains its reservation. These are accounting controls, not exact tokenization or guaranteed billing ceilings. The ledger has no configured price table, and monetary cost remains unknown. Admission and start-rate limits are process-local; the supported configuration uses one worker.

Recorded by the 27 October hardening pass. Regression counts; neither coverage percentages nor factual-accuracy measurements.

The suite exercises malformed model JSON, invalid reconciliation items, poisoned and irrelevant evidence, dropped negation, conditional claims, contradictions, partial failures, cancellation, idempotency, restart handling and provider budgets. Deterministic fixtures isolate policy behavior from live model variability.

The Northstar Coffee screenshots use one of those fixtures: supplied supplier concentration, a simulated disruption and unresolved competitive and legal evidence. A 100% module-coverage readout means each domain produced usable output. It does not mean the business has been fully researched.

A historical live run, and what it does not measure

A recorded investigation on 5 October 2026 produced 54 structured findings and 199 source records in about 389.7 seconds, with usable output from all nine specialist stages. Its specialist and synthesis snapshots reported an aggregate 1,068,479 tokens, including input/output and retry accounting.

This predates the latest hardening. The records do not establish unique source count, current latency, factual accuracy, verified spending or one million generated output tokens. Repeated context remains a performance concern. The private report itself is not published here.

Open engineering work

  • Evidence evaluation. Reviewed real-world labels, source attribution and semantic entailment; measuring abstention alongside false claims.
  • Risk calibration. Severity and likelihood are estimates. Current scores have no demonstrated calibration against business outcomes.
  • Public access. Real authentication, report ownership, tenant isolation and abuse-resistant entitlements are still required.
  • Execution at scale. Durable stage resume, worker leases and distributed admission are not implemented.
  • Provider economics. Measured context reduction, price configuration and billing reconciliation remain open.
